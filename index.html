Algorithms Used

01 · Dijkstra's Algorithm

Finds minimum-distance routes in a weighted graph with non-negative road costs.

O((V + E) log V)
02 · Merge Sort

Sorts reachable shelters by route cost using divide and conquer.

O(n log n)
03 · Greedy Allocation

Assigns each group to the nearest shelter that has sufficient remaining capacity.

O(G × S)
